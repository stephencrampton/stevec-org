              TREES

          Tall and twin
      outside my windowsill
         stand two trees
      shaking off the wind.
         One looks down
      on huddled creatures
   squirreling away nut-seeds,
     and makes an accounting
          of potential
           lives lost.

       The other gazes high
         above the town,
   beyond pedestrian affairs.
       Its roots reach low,
   but its branches extend out
        like crazy wires,
   streaming energy to the air
in crackling, Morse-code messages.
